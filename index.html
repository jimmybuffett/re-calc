    Add a house after a showing, paste the Zillow or Compass link, and rate each criterion together.

    How the score works. Each criterion's two ratings are averaged. Averages are combined using priority multipliers (priority 1 counts 5×, priority 5 counts 1×), then mapped so all 1s scores 1 and all 10s scores 100. Unrated criteria are left out until someone rates them.
